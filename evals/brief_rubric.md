# SokoIQ Brief Quality Rubric

Score each criterion 0-2. Max score: 10.

1. **Recommendation clarity** (0-2): Is the BUY/HOLD/SELL/NEUTRAL backed by at least one specific data point from the filing, market, or news data?
   - 0: No data referenced
   - 1: Vague reference ("company is performing well")
   - 2: Specific numbers cited (revenue growth %, return %, sentiment score)

2. **Financials accuracy** (0-2): Does the financials summary reflect the extracted filing data?
   - 0: Generic or contradicts filing data
   - 1: Plausible but unverifiable
   - 2: Mentions revenue/profit growth percentages matching the filing excerpt

3. **Market data cited** (0-2): Does the market summary reference specific numbers?
   - 0: No numbers
   - 1: Mentions trend ("bullish") without numbers
   - 2: Cites return percentage and/or current price

4. **Risk specificity** (0-2): Are risks specific to this company and sector?
   - 0: Fully generic ("market risk", "economic uncertainty")
   - 1: Partially specific (mentions sector but not company)
   - 2: Company-specific risks drawn from filing or news

5. **Thesis coherence** (0-2): Does the investment thesis logically connect financials, market performance, and news?
   - 0: Thesis contradicts the data or is entirely generic
   - 1: Partially coherent, missing one data dimension
   - 2: All three dimensions (financial, market, news) woven into the thesis

## Scoring

| Score | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| 8-10  | PASS  |
| 6-7   | MARGINAL |
| 0-5   | FAIL  |

A brief must score >= 8 to be considered production-quality. Three or more PASS briefs out of five companies constitutes a successful eval run.
